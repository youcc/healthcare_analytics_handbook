# Soft Skills

Technical skills get you the job. Soft skills determine how much impact you have.

Healthcare analytics engineers work at the intersection of data, clinical operations, and business strategy. Your stakeholders include physicians, nurses, revenue cycle directors, quality officers, and executives — each with different priorities, different vocabularies, and different levels of comfort with data. The ability to translate between the technical and the clinical, navigate organizational complexity, and communicate uncertainty honestly is what separates analysts who are trusted from those who are merely tolerated.

The chapters in this section address skills that are rarely taught in data programs but are critical in healthcare: how to collaborate with clinical stakeholders who are skeptical of analytics, how to manage projects in environments with long approval cycles, how to turn a vague business question into a well-scoped analysis, and how to navigate the ethical responsibilities that come with working on data that affects patient care.
